How to Play

🕹️Move

WASD or arrow keys

🖱️Look around

Drag with the mouse. Scroll to zoom.

✋Interact

Walk close to glowing things and press E

🧩Code

Drag blocks into your program, then press Run Code

📍Find your way

Follow the glowing beacon and the arrow on the mission card

🌙Moon jump

Press Space — gravity is weaker here!

⛏️Mine for stars

Click or press E next to a glowing crystal rock

🚙Drive the rover

Press E at the rover, WS drive, AD steer

🧠Code quiz

Tap 🧠 and guess what the code prints for +50 ⭐
